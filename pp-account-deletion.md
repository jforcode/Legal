# Account Deletion Request

Last updated: April 26, 2026

This page explains how users of **MyCryptogram** by **Jforcode** can
request deletion of their account and associated data.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## How to Request Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your account using one of the following
methods:

### In-App Method

1.  Open the MyCryptogram app\
2.  Go to **Settings**\
3.  Tap **Delete Account**\
4.  Your email app will open with a pre-filled request\
5.  Send the email to complete your request

### Email Method

If you cannot access the app:

1.  Send an email to **jforcode@gmail.com**\
2.  Use the subject: **Account Deletion Request**\
3.  Include your registered email address

We will process your request within **7 days**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## What Data Will Be Deleted

Upon successful account deletion, the following data will be permanently
removed:

-   Account information (such as email, if applicable)\
-   In-app progress and gameplay data\
-   Any user-specific stored data associated with your account

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## What Data May Be Retained

Some data may be retained for a limited period for the following
reasons:

-   Legal and regulatory compliance\
-   Fraud prevention and security\
-   Transaction records (if purchases were made via Google Play)

Such data will be retained only as long as necessary and then deleted.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Important Notes

-   If your account is managed via third-party services (e.g., Google
    Play), some data may also be subject to their policies\
-   Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone

------------------------------------------------------------------------
