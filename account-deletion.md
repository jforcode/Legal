# Account Deletion Request

Last updated: April 26, 2026

This page explains how users of **MyCryptogram** by **Jforcode** can request deletion of their account and associated data.

---

## How to Request Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your account by:

1. Sending an email to: jforcode@gmail.com  
2. Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request"  
3. Include your registered email address  

We will process your request within 7 days.

---

## What Data Will Be Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data will be permanently removed:

- Account information (email, profile data)
- In-app progress and usage data

---

## What Data May Be Retained

We may retain certain data if required for:

- Legal obligations
- Fraud prevention
- Security purposes

Such data will be retained only for as long as necessary.
